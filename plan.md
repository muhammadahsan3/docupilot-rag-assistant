## 2. Document Corpus

DocuPilot v1 uses five publicly available FAA Advisory Circulars
covering aviation maintenance records, maintenance manuals,
maintenance review boards, preventive maintenance, and airworthiness
directives.

### Corpus

1. AC 43-9D — Maintenance Records and FAA Form 8130-3 Return to Service
2. AC 20-77B — Use of Manufacturers' Maintenance Manuals
3. AC 121-22D — Maintenance Review Boards, Maintenance Type Boards,
   and OEM/TCH Recommended Maintenance Procedures
4. AC 43-12A — Preventive Maintenance
5. AC 39-7D — Airworthiness Directives

The original PDF documents are stored locally in `data/raw/` and
are excluded from Git version control.

The documents are publicly available through the Federal Aviation
Administration.
